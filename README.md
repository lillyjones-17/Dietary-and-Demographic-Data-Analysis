## Project Overview
The purpose of this data analysis is to understand the characteristics and dietary factors and their relationships between one another based on the “Cancer Risk” dataset. This analysis uses descriptive statistics, measures of center and dispersion, tables, graphs, and analytical tools to examine the variables and identify their relationships. 

## Variables
The five variables that were examined across 310 observations were age, gender, smoking status, daily calorie intake, and daily fat intake. 

## Methods
The following statistical and analytical methods were used:

Descriptive statistics and frequency distributions

Data categorization and group comparisons

Histograms, boxplots, scatterplots, and stacked bar charts

Contingency tables

Confidence interval estimation

## Key Findings
The mean age of participants was approximately 50.19 years.

The average daily calorie intake was approximately 1,796.7 calories.

Participants aged 35 and younger had the highest average daily fat intake.

A slight negative trend was observed between age and daily calorie intake.
